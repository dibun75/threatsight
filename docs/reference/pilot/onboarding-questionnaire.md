# Onboarding questionnaire

Operational questions — not a signed agreement.

1. Primary technical contact and backup contact?
2. Preferred deployment: hardened Compose or Helm?
3. PostgreSQL version, TLS mode, and backup destination class?
4. OIDC issuer, client id, redirect URI, group → role mapping?
5. How many recovery administrators?
6. Named tenant and workspace identifiers?
7. Which 3–5 business services are in scope?
8. Azure/Entra app registration owner?
9. Edge source (Meraki / Junos / none for week 1)?
10. Where will the customer runner run?
11. Data-retention expectation (days/months)?
12. Maintenance window preference?
13. Who approves readiness actions?
14. Export / screenshot policy for demos?
15. Offboarding date or review checkpoint?
